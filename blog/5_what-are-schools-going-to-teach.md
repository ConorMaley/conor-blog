## We don't need our CS students spending years worth of time and money on learning Java

Hopefully no CS 100-199 course is teaching Java anymore lol.

Anyway, with AI tooling, what should we be focusing on teaching these kids?

### C is cool still! 

Learning a high level language is a great exercise. Every CS student should understand what a `malloc` is. They should feel the pain of memory management for the deeper understanding of what is actually happening behind the scenes of your machine!

### Turing is still important

One of my favorite classes at Wooster was CS220, Theory of Computation (no I didn't remember the number, I looked it up and they're still teaching it 10 years later! woo!)

> The theory of abstract machines and formal languages is introduced in this course. Computability by finite automata, pushdown automata, and Turing machines is examined and related to pattern matching, lexical analysis, compilation and programming for digital computer systems. Proofs by induction, construction, contradiction, and reduction are used to formalize computability theory and the limitations of computing.
